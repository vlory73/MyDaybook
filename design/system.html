Motion

  • Quick and quiet: 120 ms press, 200 ms fades, 280 ms sheets, ease-out.
  • Sheets slide up from the "+" button; they never pop in from nowhere.
  • Confirm by change, not celebration: a soft check morph, no confetti.
  • Undo toasts slide in for 5 s instead of "Are you sure?" dialogs.
  • Reduced motion: all movement becomes a short cross-fade.
